# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Phi is a tool-building community for people who work with terminal agents, Go,
and related developer tools. We want discussions and contributions around the
project to be useful, direct, and safe for everyone taking part.

This policy explains the conduct we expect from participants and the actions
maintainers may take when that standard is not met. It applies to contributors,
maintainers, users, sponsors, and anyone participating in a space connected with
phi.

## What good participation looks like

Participants are expected to:

- Treat people with patience and respect, including when a technical discussion
  becomes difficult.
- Focus criticism on the proposal, code, documentation, or behavior under
  discussion rather than on a person.
- Assume good intent while still taking responsibility for the effect of our
  words and actions.
- Give feedback that is specific, useful, and appropriate for the forum.
- Make room for different backgrounds, skill levels, languages, viewpoints, and
  ways of contributing.
- Keep shared spaces readable: provide context, avoid needless pile-ons, and
  move long side discussions to a suitable channel.
- Respect project boundaries, permissions, privacy, and the security of other
  users.
- Correct mistakes openly and cooperate with reasonable moderation requests.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Strong opinions are welcome. Personal abuse
is not required for either one.

## Conduct that is not acceptable

The following behavior is not welcome in project spaces:

- Harassment, threats, stalking, unwanted sexual attention, or discriminatory
  remarks.
- Insults, slurs, ridicule, or repeated personal attacks aimed at a person or
  group.
- Publishing private or identifying information without clear permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, flooding channels, impersonating others,
  or coordinating attacks against a participant.
- Sexualized language or imagery in a context where it is not appropriate.
- Intimidation, retaliation, or pressuring someone because they made a report,
  gave feedback, or declined an interaction.
- Misleading claims made to damage a person, project, or contributor's
  reputation.
- Submitting malicious code, unsafe tool behavior, or instructions intended
  to compromise another person's system or data.
- Ignoring a maintainer's reasonable request to stop behavior covered by this
  policy.

A contribution can be rejected for technical or project reasons without being a
conduct violation. Moderation decisions should explain the relevant project
reason when doing so will help the discussion.

## Where this applies

This policy applies to the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, documentation, release work, and the official phi
Discord server. It also applies in other settings when someone is acting as a
representative of the project, such as using a project role at an event or in a
public announcement.

Project spaces may have additional rules. Those rules continue to apply when
they provide more detail for a particular platform.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness behavior that may violate this policy, please
contact the phi maintainers privately at
[zhengel2022@163.com](mailto:zhengel2022@163.com). Include, if you can do so
safely:

- what happened and when;
- where it happened, such as an issue, pull request, or Discord channel;
- links, screenshots, or other records that help establish the context; and
- the usernames or names of people involved.

Please do not forward sensitive details more widely than necessary. You may ask
someone you trust to help prepare a report. Reports made in good faith will be
taken seriously, including reports about conduct by a maintainer.

For security vulnerabilities in phi, use the private process in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead of posting exploit details in a public
space.

## How maintainers respond

Maintainers will review reports as promptly as practical, protect the privacy
of the people involved where possible, and avoid sharing more information than
is needed to handle the matter. They may ask for clarification, consult another
maintainer, or recuse themselves when a report concerns them.

Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may:

- clarify a rule or ask for a change in behavior;
- remove or edit a comment, issue, review, commit, or other contribution;
- limit or pause participation in one or more project spaces; or
- permanently end a person's access to project spaces.

The response will consider the context, impact, frequency, and seriousness of
the behavior. Maintainers will aim for a proportionate and consistent response,
while prioritizing the safety of people affected and the health of the project.

A person who receives a moderation decision may request a review by replying to
the private decision. A request should include new context or a clear reason to
reconsider the decision. The original decision remains in effect while the
review is pending.

## Privacy and good-faith reports

We will keep reports and identities private where practical. We cannot promise
complete confidentiality when action requires sharing information, when there is
an immediate safety concern, or when disclosure is required by law.

Reports should be honest and made to resolve a real concern. Knowingly false
reports, deliberate misrepresentation, and retaliation against a reporter are
violations of this policy.

## Questions

Questions about this policy can be sent to the same maintainer contact listed
above. If a platform's moderation tools are better suited to an urgent issue,
use those tools as well as contacting the maintainers when possible.

This policy may be updated as the project and its community change. Any update
will be published in the repository.
